# TikTok Shop Partner Center — Resubmission Guide

**Purpose:** owner-operated guidance only. This document does not authorize access or submission. The exact form question number was not included in the rejection text and is intentionally not guessed.

## Before opening the rejected review

1. Confirm the official Partner Center account and project/app under review.
2. Locate the rejected Data Security and Privacy Review case and preserve its case reference privately.
3. Read the complete reviewer note and identify the exact data-protection question, answer currently selected, and accepted evidence types.
4. Compare each answer with implemented and deployed controls, not only local source code or policy text.
5. If “Yes” is unsupported, change the response to the truthful answer or complete the missing control first. Do not select Yes merely to satisfy the form.

## Resolve the specific evidence request

The rejection says the reviewer could not verify data-protection compliance from the supplied information and asks for either a Personal Information Protection Standard or proof that the relevant option was selected as Yes. This does not identify which exact question was intended. Use the actual form labels; do not infer the number or scope.

### If the question is already answered Yes

1. Verify that Yes remains selected in the current rejected application.
2. Capture a genuine screenshot showing the exact question and selection. Keep the original in protected evidence storage.
3. Confirm the response is supportable from actual controls and evidence. A screenshot of an answer proves the selection, not that the underlying control exists.
4. Attach the approved Personal Information Protection Standard and relevant evidence items from `SUBMISSION_EVIDENCE_CHECKLIST.md`.

### If the question is not answered Yes or the answer cannot be substantiated

1. Do not mark Yes.
2. Identify the missing control(s) and evidence owner.
3. Implement and verify the required controls through separately authorized work.
4. Update the policy and public disclosures so they match actual practice.
5. Re-answer the form truthfully and explain remediation in the response.

## Attachments

Attach the standard only after the owner confirms its scope, legal entity/operator, approval status, effective date, and language. Add only relevant supporting evidence; do not submit raw Cloud reports, customer records, OAuth URLs, secrets, or unredacted screenshots. Keep all attachments consistent about which controls are local, which are deployed, and which remain pending.

## Final human review

Before pressing submit, the owner should check:

- App/project identity and review case are correct.
- Each answer matches current deployed reality.
- “Yes” answers have evidence for the actual question, not an adjacent control.
- Document version, approval, effective date, public privacy/deletion URLs, and contact channel are accurate.
- No unsupported certification, legal-conformity statement, backup claim, or Shop OAuth claim is included.
- Attachments open correctly and contain no secrets or unnecessary personal information.
- A copy of the final form and submitted attachments is retained privately.

The owner performs any resubmission manually. Do not use this guide to bypass a reviewer restriction or submit automatically.
